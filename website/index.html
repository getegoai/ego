01 / The direction

An AI companion that grows with you.

Understand unfamiliar things. Take the next step. Carry longer-term goals forward within clear permissions. Ego’s ambition is to expand what one person can accomplish.

Our design direction is local first, with clear data flows and user control. Each release should explain what works, what has been tested, and what is still being built.

02 / The projects

Two connected starting points.

[ 01 ]Wearable device

EgoLink

A connection to the physical world.

Exploring location, motion sensing, cellular communication, and a physical SOS interaction. Current work focuses on repeatable daily testing, practical improvements, and documenting the prototype’s limits.

STATUS / ENGINEERING PROTOTYPEHardware and software are in development. Product validation is incomplete.

[ 02 ]Local hub

EgoBase

A home for your personal context.

Being developed to receive device events, keep personal context, and support longer-running tasks. Initial work uses existing computers and a software data core while we test how the pieces fit together.

03 / The roadmap

Small steps. Real progress.

01 — IN PROGRESS

Now

Publish the project direction and current implementation. Bring EgoLink prototypes into repeatable daily testing, with clear records of what works and what fails.

02 — UP NEXT

Next

Connect EgoLink to a minimal local EgoBase experience. Improve reliability, power use, data handling, and the tools for reproducing problems and contributing.

03 — EXPLORING

Later

Explore richer personal context, longer-running assistance, and coordination across devices and people. Let practical use guide the product.

This is a direction, not a shipping schedule.
